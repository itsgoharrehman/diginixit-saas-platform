Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct insights into our technology law frameworks and compliance sprint processes.

Why do tech startups need specialized technology legal advisory instead of general law firms? ↓

General corporate lawyers often lack technical comprehension of cloud sub-processors, open-source copyleft licenses (GPL/AGPL), API data flows, and modern cookie consent scripts. We bridge jurisprudence and code architecture directly.

How does your SaaS MSA accelerate enterprise procurement cycles? ↓

Enterprise deals frequently stall during procurement redlines due to overly aggressive or amateur liability clauses. We structure balanced, industry-standard MSAs, SLAs, and DPAs that enterprise legal departments can approve quickly without months of friction.

What constitutes complete intellectual property ring-fencing for software? ↓

It requires irrevocable IP assignment agreements from all founders, employees, and freelance contractors, backed by clean open-source licensing audits and verified repository copyright notices, ensuring zero ownership clouds during venture capital due diligence.

What privacy jurisdictions do your compliance audits cover? ↓

We evaluate platforms under the European Union GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA, the UK Data Protection Act, and relevant international cross-border data transfer frameworks.

What is the turnaround time for a comprehensive compliance audit? ↓

A standard compliance audit sprint is executed within 5 to 7 business days, delivering an actionable threat matrix, remediation code recommendations, and custom contract drafts.
