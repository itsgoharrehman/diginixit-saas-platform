The Real Challenge

Why Product Interfaces Fail to Convert

When user interfaces are treated merely as visual mockups, products suffer from high bounce rates, developer frustration, and inconsistent brand experiences.

01

High Onboarding Drop-Off

Convoluted registration flows and cognitive overload in user setup screens cause 40%+ of new signups to abandon platforms before reaching their core aha-moment.

02

Developer Handoff Friction

Disorganized design files without responsive auto-layout or systematic tokens force developers to guess padding, font weights, and edge cases, ballooning sprint hours.

03

Accessibility Violations

Low-contrast text, missing focus states, and unlabelled interactive controls alienate users with assistive devices and expose enterprise brands to statutory legal risks.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on our design tools, methodologies, and developer collaboration models.

How do you ensure Figma designs translate accurately into code? ↓

We build design systems with automated design tokens (colors, spacing, typography, radii) that map 1:1 to CSS variables and Tailwind configuration. Developers receive exact specifications, eliminating visual drift and handoff disputes.

Are your designs compliant with accessibility standards (WCAG)? ↓

Yes. Every interface is designed to meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines, with verified color contrast ratios, distinct focus indicators, and logical keyboard navigation paths.

Do you design for mobile apps as well as web applications? ↓

Yes. We design responsive web applications that seamlessly scale from ultra-wide 4K displays down to mobile viewports, as well as native iOS and Android mobile interfaces with platform-standard design patterns.

What deliverables do we receive at the end of a design project? ↓

You receive a complete, organized Figma file containing the component library, interactive high-fidelity prototypes, user journey diagrams, responsive screen layouts, design tokens export, and developer handoff documentation.

How long does a typical product design sprint take? ↓

A targeted UI/UX overhaul or new MVP design sprint typically takes 2 to 4 weeks. Multi-product enterprise design systems are executed across iterative sprints with continuous stakeholder reviews.
