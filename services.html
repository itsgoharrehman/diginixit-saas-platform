03 — Growth

Technical SEO & Search Architecture Engineering

Search engine dominance is an engineering problem, not a copywriting trick. Google rewards platforms that excel in crawl efficiency, sub-second rendering, comprehensive Schema.org knowledge graphs, and verifiable topical authority. We engineer your digital properties to eliminate indexation bottlenecks and outrank entrenched competitors across high-intent search queries.

Core SEO Engineering Scope

Core Web Vitals Optimization

Systematic elimination of render-blocking resources, font lag, and layout shifts to achieve green-tier scores on LCP, INP, and CLS.

Schema.org Knowledge Graphs

Custom nested JSON-LD structured data (@graph) that defines your entities, services, authors, and organizations for rich snippet eligibility.

Crawl Budget & Indexing Audits

Resolution of canonical conflicts, redirect chains, server header anomalies, and crawler bloat to ensure search bots index your high-value pages.

Topical Authority Architecture

Content pillar modeling and internal link clustering designed to demonstrate deep subject-matter expertise across competitive search topics.

Problems We Solve

  • Stagnant Organic Rankings: Pinpointing technical deficiencies preventing your best content from ranking on page 1.
  • Algorithmic Penalties & Core Update Volatility: Aligning site structure with Google's helpful content and technical quality guidelines.
  • Single-Page App (SPA) Crawl Failures: Implementing edge server-side rendering so dynamic content is instantly visible to search bots.
Methodology Deep Crawl → Schema Design → Code Fixes → Rank Compounding

We don't hand you an overwhelming PDF report and walk away. Our engineers directly implement the code, server headers, and structured data changes necessary to achieve measurable ranking lifts.

We bridge the gap between software engineering and regulatory jurisprudence—delivering actionable compliance audits, bulletproof commercial SaaS contracts, and ironclad intellectual property assignment frameworks designed to withstand institutional due diligence.

Legal & Compliance Engineering Scope

GDPR & CCPA Privacy Audits

Comprehensive mapping of user data lifecycles, cross-border transfer mechanisms, cookie consent architecture, and privacy policies to eliminate statutory enforcement risk.

SaaS MSAs & Enterprise SLAs

Enterprise-grade master service agreements, custom licensing contracts, uptime commitments, and balanced liability caps structured to accelerate B2B procurement.

IP Ring-Fencing & Copyright

Unambiguous assignment of software source code, proprietary algorithms, trade secrets, and trademark registrations to secure company enterprise value.

Third-Party & AI Risk Governance

Rigorous vetting of cloud sub-processors, open-source license compliance (MIT, Apache, GPL copyleft audits), and emerging AI training data policies.

Methodology Threat Audit → Contract Engineering → Privacy Workflows → Continuous Governance
