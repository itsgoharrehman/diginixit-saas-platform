Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about our engineering methodology, intellectual property rights, security standards, and client engagement framework.

How does DiginixIT differ from traditional web development agencies?

Traditional agencies often rely on bloated CMS templates, drag-and-drop page builders, and fragmented freelance contractors who build fragile MVPs that fail under load. DiginixIT operates as an engineering and strategy partner. We write custom, type-safe code, engineer edge-rendered architectures, and build on scalable database schemas from day one.

Furthermore, we eliminate the blind spots of technology by integrating certified technology law compliance (GDPR/CCPA/IP protection) and corporate financial modeling directly into our product strategy, ensuring your venture is built for enterprise longevity and investor scrutiny.

Who owns the source code, design systems, and intellectual property?

You do — 100%. Upon project delivery and milestone fulfillment, all intellectual property, custom code repositories, UI/UX design components, database schemas, and documentation are transferred fully to your ownership.

We practice zero vendor lock-in. Your code is clean, thoroughly documented, and structured according to standard industry conventions so your internal developers or future hires can seamlessly maintain and expand it.

How do you guarantee top-tier Core Web Vitals and sub-second load times?

We engineer every web application from the critical rendering path up. We utilize global edge content delivery, server-side caching, critical CSS prioritization, font subsetting, and modern image formats (WebP/AVIF). We write zero-bloat JavaScript, avoiding third-party script pollution that destroys browser responsiveness.

Before any production deployment, we benchmark Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), Interaction to Next Paint (INP), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) on real mobile and desktop profiles to guarantee green-tier performance in Google's ranking criteria.

Can DiginixIT integrate with our existing backend, APIs, or legacy databases?

Yes. Our team specializes in complex integrations and platform modernization. We build custom API gateways, webhook processors, authentication pipelines (OAuth2, Supabase Auth, JWT), and secure database connections across PostgreSQL, MySQL, REST, and GraphQL architectures.

Whether you need to migrate from legacy architecture without downtime or connect mission-critical payment gateways like Stripe, we ensure seamless data synchronization and zero data loss.

What is your typical project timeline and collaboration model?

We operate through transparent, milestone-driven sprints. Focused architectural sprints and technical SEO performance overhauls typically span 2 to 4 weeks. Complete bespoke SaaS platforms and enterprise applications generally require 6 to 12 weeks depending on scope complexity.

Every engagement starts with a structured discovery call, clear fixed-scope deliverables, transparent milestone schedules, and direct access to senior engineers and strategists.
